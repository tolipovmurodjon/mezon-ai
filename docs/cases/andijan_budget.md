## 2. The rule it broke (as reported)

- **Budget Code art. 99** + the E-qaror regulation (VMQ No. 390, 22.06.2021): district and city hokimiyats post local budget drafts on the public-discussion portal **by 10 October**, revise them on the comments, and resubmit to the councils **by 1 November** (per Daryo). The Budget Code is on lex.uz -2304138; the **exact article text is to be confirmed by the lawyer.**
- The 2,206 "public discussion violations on documents for additional local budget sources" found by Adliya's E-qaror monitoring (Kun.uz 19.12.2022) show this is a known, repeated pattern.

## 3. The same pattern two years earlier (2023 budgets)

Gazeta.uz, from E-qaror + regulation.gov.uz data, via Anhor 09.01.2023:
- "practically all" hokimiyats approved 2023 budgets without taxpayers' input;
- only **2** posted drafts (Andijan city, Sirdaryo region), and **both approved before their discussion closed** (Andijan city 31.12.2022 vs deadline 04.01.2023; Sirdaryo 30.12.2022);
- Samarkand region approved 29.12.2022, Namangan region 30.12.2022, and Tashkent city Kengash 04.01.2023.
