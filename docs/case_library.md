# Mezon case library: big numbers, strongest cases, talking points

Built 18 Nov 2026 from public sources. The full rows (37) are in `data/knowledge/case_library.csv` and the laws in
`data/knowledge/core_laws.csv`. Deep dossiers: `docs/cases/designcode.md`, `docs/cases/andijan_budget.md`.
Rules: legality, not corruption; bodies, not people; "possible violation — flagged for expert review", never "illegal"
unless a court or adliya already said so. Most numbers are official figures reported by the press: say the source.

## (b) The 10 strongest cases

1. **Qarshi district, school micro-districts (231-4-59-Q/24, 21.06.2024).**
   - *What:* a word-for-word copy of 207-4-59-Q/24 adopted 15 days earlier and still in force; its clause 4 repeals an act
     that 207 had already repealed. E-qaror shows public discussion and legal expertise both skipped.
   - *Harm:* two acts in force on one matter for every family with school-age children.
   - *Caught:* district adliya representation №27-7/1-34 (03.07.2024), then cancelled 15.07.2024.
   - *Mezon:* check 7 (duplicate, double repeal) + check 10 (skipped stage) on adoption day, **12 days before adliya**.
     Source: lex.uz -7026714.
2. **Yangiyo‘l district Kengash, land tax (VI-76-4-10-146-K/24, 11.01.2024).**
   - *What:* coefficients 0.7–3.0 for every individual and company, adopted without public discussion (PQ-3666 item 4).
   - *Caught:* district adliya letter No. 2 (09.01.2024); cancelled and re-adopted 06.03.2024.
   - *Mezon:* check 10 (normative decision, discussion stage grey on E-qaror) on adoption day. There is a control pair: the
     re-adopted version shows the stage done. Source: lex.uz -6832596.
3. **Tashkent city Kengash design-code (17.02.2026).**
   - *What:* facade, sign and colour rules for businesses, adopted without the mandatory Business Ombudsman agreement and
     with undefined norms.
   - *Harm:* 3,228 businesses in the rollout; ~20 mln so‘m per sign removal reported by one chain.
   - *Caught:* Ombudsman conclusion 25.04.2026, after adoption.
   - *Mezon:* checks 3 + 12 at Gate 0 when the draft was posted (~29.12.2025), **~50 days before adoption and ~139 days
     before entry into force**. Source: Spot.uz (25.04.2026); full dossier in `docs/cases/designcode.md`.
4. **Yakkabog‘ district, farm land (20-4-68-Q/22, 26.01.2022).**
   - *What:* revoked a dairy farm's land allocation (project >50 % done) **after PF-6243 had removed district hokims' power
     over land rights from 01.08.2021**.
   - *Caught:* Ombudsman → Qarshi administrative court; declared invalid 30.03.2022.
   - *Mezon:* check 2 (competence on the decision date). Source: Kun.uz (11.04.2022).
5. **Sirdaryo: a zombie tax.**
   - *What:* a court annulled a hokim's 11.2 ha decision (Nov 2023), but land tax kept being charged because the cadastre
     record wasn't updated.
   - *Harm:* 645.3 mln + 129 mln so‘m in fines.
   - *Caught:* Ombudsman → Guliston administrative court; the tax decision was declared invalid.
   - *Mezon:* check 8 (agencies still acting on a cancelled decision). Source: UzA (21.10.2026).
6. **Olot district, a decision that passed legal expertise and was still cancelled (362-2-31-Q/22).**
   - *What:* E-qaror shows both public discussion and legal expertise *done*, yet the district adliya cancelled it by
     representation (25.10.2022).
   - *Mezon:* this is the "even the expertise stage let it through" proof (N = 1, our observation). Source: lex.uz -6273670
     + E-qaror stage tracker.
7. **Tashkent city Kengash outdoor-advertising decision (27.03.2021).**
   - *What:* adopted **without adliya legal expertise** (NLA arts. 21–22, per the Ombudsman), while structures were being
     dismantled on its basis.
   - *Mezon:* check 3 / stage tracker. Source: Kun.uz (28.05.2021).
8. **Andijan region Kengashes, 2025 local budgets (27–28.12.2024).**
   - *What:* posted for discussion the same day they were adopted; the discussion was to run until 11.01.2025. In
     2023, "practically all" hokimiyats approved budgets without public input (Gazeta.uz, 09.01.2023).
   - *Mezon:* check 9 (adopted before the discussion ended). Source: Daryo (single report; re-read in a browser);
     `docs/cases/andijan_budget.md`.
9. **Guliston, 34 shops (Jan 2026).**
   - *What:* shops on land allocated by a 2007 city decision were demolished; the entrepreneurs say there was no court
     decision and no compensation, while the hokimlik says they were warned and offered relocation.
   - *Mezon:* land-vertical checklist (public-need purpose under art. 4, compensation, court route). Source: Kun.uz
     (08.01.2026).
10. **Draft 118221, Andijan region Kengash household-waste tariffs (open now).**
    - *What:* entry into force 01.12.2026 is set *before* its public discussion ends (02.12.2026); the clause numbering
      skips 4; 0 public proposals.
    - *Mezon:* check 9 + check 13, live at Gate 0, today. The lawyer must confirm before any filing. Source:
      regulation.gov.uz/oz/d/118221.

## (c) Talking points

**Adliya jury**
- "Your own monitoring found 986 wrong-form orders and 2,206 public-discussion violations (19.12.2022). Each one was
  found after adoption. Mezon finds them on the day they are posted."
- "We don't replace the expert: every flag cites the law, the article and a quote, in your own conclusion format
  (positive / with fixable defects / negative, order 3333). The expert decides (rules No. 3787)."
- "LexAI answers questions, E-huquqshunos drafts, KOISA ($14.5 m) builds AI legal services. Nobody independently checks
  every local decision against the law as it read that day. That is the slot Mezon fills, as a pilot component."
- "It works today on your public data: E-qaror's stage tracker, lex.uz texts, regulation.gov.uz drafts."
- **Avoid:** "your experts miss things". Say "the volume is too big for manual review: about 23 new drafts a day on the
  portal, most of them local."

**Business Ombudsman**
- "The design-code had no Ombudsman agreement, and you found it in April. Mezon flags every business-affecting local
  draft with no Ombudsman step, on the day it is posted."
- "Mezon can triage your intake: an appeal arrives with the decision, the law and the article attached."
- "Zombie obligations like Sirdaryo (tax charged on a cancelled decision) become a standing check."
- Ask for: 1–2 anonymised example cases, a one-line quote, a letter of intent for a pilot. Endorsement only in writing.

**District adliya expert (interview)**
- "Show me the last draft you sent back. Why? How long did it take?"
- The four baseline questions: drafts per month · share of negative or fixable-defect conclusions · minutes per draft ·
  how many positively concluded decisions were later cancelled.
- "If a tool pre-filled the conclusion with articles and quotes, what would you need to trust it?"

**Hokimlik lawyer**
- "Mezon protects the hokim's own name: it's a pre-check before the draft goes to adliya, so fewer decisions get
  cancelled in court."
- "Duplicates, double repeals, skipped stages and wrong form are mechanical errors. Mezon catches them before signing."

**Business owner**
- "You learn about a decision when the inspector arrives. Mezon tells you the day it is published whether it affects you
  and whether it may conflict with the law."
- "If it does, you get a ready appeal to the right channel (Ombudsman, adliya, Kengash, court). You decide whether to send it."
- **Never** promise an outcome or call a specific decision illegal unless a court or adliya already did.

## Positioning: Adliya's own AI and digital plans

- **KOISA project:** $14.5 m for AI-based legal services and a Korea–Uzbekistan law center; the plan period is 2026–2030
  per one UzA item; the dates in the sources conflict (UzA; gov.uz/adliya).
- **AI ethics rules No. 3787** (registered 14.03.2026; effective 17.06.2026 per UzA): legality, transparency, and no
  decisions based only on AI conclusions.
- **LexAI** (a legal Q&A chatbot) and "Law as Code" (machine-readable legislation) are Ministry initiatives (UzA / spot.uz).
- **Mezon's slot:** a document-level, time-aware legality check of local decisions, which none of these cover.
