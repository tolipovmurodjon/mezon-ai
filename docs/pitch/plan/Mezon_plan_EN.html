3
How it works

AI proposes. The verifier checks. A person signs.

Data flows down the page. Nothing reaches a citizen or an expert without passing the verifier, and nothing becomes a conclusion without a signature.

built and running being built this week the agent (model connects on Friday)

1 · AI proposes

The agent reads a decision and calls tools: the 14 rule checks, a lookup of the law as it read on the decision date, a search, the parent act. Every step is written to a trace the expert can open.

2 · The verifier checks

Every quote must appear verbatim in the edition in force that day. No article, no quote, no edition: no flag. If nothing can be cited, the status is "unclear, needs expert".

3 · A person signs

The expert gets a draft conclusion in Adliya's own format (positive · positive with fixable defects · negative) and decides each flag. Mezon sends nothing by itself.

Opens the queue: what changed, and what is flagged

"Adliya navbati" (Adliya queue). The top panel, "Qonun o'zgardi" (The law changed), lists decisions in force whose article was amended later. That is not a violation: it means the decision should be re-read against the new edition. Below it, the flagged decisions, riskiest and newest first.

Opens one decision: the dead-article flag

355-4-68-Q/25, Yakkabog' district hokim, 13.11.2025. Mezon ran 9 checks. Seven found nothing, one did not apply, and one raised a flag (the pink tile).

The decision says it is adopted "on the basis of articles 6 and 25" of the Law on Local State Power. Article 25 had lost force under O'RQ-976 of 17.10.2024, more than a year earlier.

Left, in ember: the quote from the decision. Right, in teal: lex.uz's own note on article 25, and the rule that applies (NLA Law O'RQ-682, art. 18 part 3, as it read on 13.11.2025).

Status: "possible violation, flagged for expert review". Under the flag the expert presses To'g'ri / Noto'g'ri / Noaniq (correct / wrong / unclear).

4

Gets a draft conclusion to edit and sign

"Xulosa loyihasi" (Draft conclusion) is written in the format of Adliya order No. 3333. Proposed result: "positive with fixable defects". Each defect carries the quote from the decision, the lex.uz status and the legal basis, followed by the 29-factor anti-corruption checklist (O'RQ-860) and blank fields for the expert's decision and signature. "Word'da ochish" opens the same text in Word.

5

Asks Mezon AI as an expert and sees how the agent checked

In "Mezon AI" the expert picks "Adliya eksperti" and asks about the decision. The answer gives the same flag with both quotes, and "Mezon qanday tekshirdi" (How Mezon checked) lists the 8 steps the agent took.

Each step is a tool call with its result: read the decision text, ran 9 rules, checked whether article 6 was in force on 13.11.2025 (yes), then article 25 (no: lost force).

Today these answers come from the rules and the law store; the local language model is still off. Connecting Qwen is Friday's job.

The headline says it plainly: a new obligation for businesses takes effect less than 3 months after the decision, so a business would face a new requirement or fee without the preparation period the law gives. Mezon found 2 possible defects.

The black box names the channel: the Business Ombudsman (and the district adliya), with a ready appeal text to copy. The owner sends it; Mezon sends nothing automatically.

Labels are the exam

The 287 positive labels come from the text of the cancelling act: an adliya representation, a prosecutor's protest, a court ruling, or E-qaror "declared invalid". We score Mezon on them; we do not train on them.

Recall needs text

Most "declared invalid" rows in E-qaror have stages but no text, so text rules are scored only on positives that have text. We always print both N.

Samples are samples

"87 of 92" is our random sample of recent drafts, said as a sample, never as an official statistic.

6
Our own model: Mezon-LM

Train up to date X, take everything after X from a daily change log

The key idea

The model is trained on all data up to date X and learns how to work. Every change after X is written to a daily change log; before answering, the agent checks the log and the lex.uz link first, and the newest information always wins. Every 2-3 months (or monthly) the log is folded in and the model is re-trained.

Exam first

A public benchmark of real questions with lawyer-checked answers: point-in-time articles, real cancelled decisions, law changes. Nothing is "better" until it beats this.

Baseline with no training

Qwen 27B plus the tools and the verifier. Pick embeddings (bge-m3 or multilingual-e5) by a 20-question Uzbek test.

Train up to date X

LoRA on lex.uz laws and decisions, court rulings and official statistics (the parts whose license we checked); then on lawyer-confirmed flags and signed conclusions. Kept only if it beats the baseline on the exam.

Daily change log

Every day a fast algorithm diffs the new editions on lex.uz: added, changed and removed articles go into a history store. The agent checks that store before answering and gives the lex.uz link.

Refresh every 2-3 months (or monthly)

Re-train with the changes in the log; run the exam again. Date X moves forward.

Quantize for any office

4-bit or FP8, so a regional server can run it. Decision text never leaves government machines.

Open it

Weights and the benchmark on Hugging Face, so anyone can check there is no hidden cloud API. Datasets only after the terms are agreed with lex.uz's owner.

Rules we never break

Six lines that hold the whole project

01Flagged is not illegal. We say "possible violation, flagged for expert review".
02A human decides every flag (Adliya AI ethics rules No. 3787).
03No flag without an article, a verbatim quote and the edition of that day.
04Decision text never goes to a cloud AI. Everything runs on our machines.
05No endorsement claims from Adliya or the Ombudsman unless in writing.
06Polite crawling only: 1 request per second, public pages, no bot-check bypass.
