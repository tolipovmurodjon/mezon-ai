# LexAI test, 19.11.2026 (ai.lex.uz, Chat-bot-2, free requests, Boss's account)

Purpose: see what Adliya's own chatbot does with the two questions only Mezon is built for. Respectful use only: we
show this as "the step Mezon automates", never as an attack.

## Q1 (point in time)
"Mahalliy davlat hokimiyati to'g'risidagi Qonunning 25-moddasi 2025-yil 13-noyabr holatida amalda bo'lganmi? Agar tuman
hokimining qarori shu moddaga asoslanib qabul qilingan bo'lsa, buning huquqiy oqibati qanday?"

LexAI (summary): it could not find whether article 25 was in force on that date and asked which edition was meant; its
retrieved documents held only article 7 of that law. It then reasoned correctly in general: a decision resting on a
lost article is weakened but not cancelled automatically (NLA art. 45 routes), and advised the user to check the
edition of that date on lex.uz themselves.
Correct answer (lex.uz): article 25 lost force under O'RQ-976 of 17.10.2024 (from 19.10.2024). Mezon shows lex.uz's own
note verbatim.

## Q2 (audit a real decision)
"Yakkabog' tumani hokimining 2025-yil 13-noyabrdagi 355-4-68-Q/25-sonli qarori qaysi qonun moddalariga asoslangan va bu
asos qabul qilingan sanada to'g'ri bo'lganmi?"

LexAI (summary): it does not have the decision's text, so it could not say which articles it rests on; it asked the user
to paste the preamble. It gave the right general rule (NLA art. 16 part 3) and a manual checklist: write out every cited
article, then check each one's edition of 13.11.2025 on lex.uz.
Mezon: reads the decision, finds "Qonunining 6 va 25-moddalariga asosan", checks both articles on 13.11.2025, flags
article 25 with lex.uz's note, and drafts the conclusion.

## The pitch line
LexAI itself tells the user what to do next: check each cited article's edition on lex.uz. Mezon does exactly that,
automatically, for every local decision, every day. "LexAI javob beradi; Mezon tekshiradi." They fit together.
