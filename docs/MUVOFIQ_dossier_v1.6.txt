0 · ELI5: the whole idea in simple words
Read this first if you are new to the project, or before explaining it to anyone.
The problem, like you are five
Real world
Simple picture
Laws, PQ/PF decrees
The big rulebook for the whole country.
Hokim decision
A local rule one district boss writes, like a school principal writing rules for one
school.
The law says
A principal may NOT write a rule that breaks the big rulebook.
What goes wrong
Sometimes the local rule goes too far. The big rule says “keep the streets clean”;
the local rule says “we will enter your house with a camera to check it is clean”.
That second part is in no rulebook.
Who checks today
Adliya checks local rules — but by hand, against 34,000+ laws, with little time.
Some bad rules slip through.
What happens then
People lose land, money or peace. Much later a court says “this rule was illegal”
and cancels it (933 times in 2024 alone). Too late for the people already hurt.
The solution, like you are five
MUVOFIQ is a spell-checker for laws. Grammarly underlines mistakes in your English.
MUVOFIQ underlines the parts of a local decision that do not match the big rulebook — and
shows the exact page of the rulebook.

Why our own computers
Government drafts are like private letters. We read them at home (our own server) and never
send them to foreign AI services.
How we prove it works
Like checking homework with the answers at the back of the book: we test MUVOFIQ on old
decisions where a court already said “illegal”. If MUVOFIQ finds the same problem the court
found, it works. We show that score honestly.
Why they should fund us
Like planting a seed: give us one region for 90 days. We show real numbers (decisions checked,
time saved, errors caught). If it grows, plant it in every region through E-qaror, the system Adliya
already runs.
The whole pitch in three sentences
1. Local decisions sometimes break the law, and today they are checked by hand, so many are
caught only in court.
2. MUVOFIQ compares each decision with the decree it claims to follow and the law in force that
day, and marks every problem with its source.
3. A human lawyer decides; we make them faster (we measure exactly how much) and catch
problems before anyone is hurt.

1 · Read this first: five fixes to our pitch plan
These five changes decide whether we win or get disqualified. The jury is the Ministry of Justice,
TDYU law professors, IT Park and AI Alliance. They are lawyers and officials who check every
claim.
FIX 1 — Never invent traction
 Do NOT say “we tested and found 3,000 illegal decisions, approved by top lawyers” unless it
really happened. In two days it cannot happen. One question from a professor (“which lawyers?
show the list”) ends the pitch.
 Say the honest, still-strong version: “On a test set of N decisions, we caught X of Y that courts
later cancelled. A lawyer reviewed Z of our flags; P% were correct.” Real small numbers beat fake
big ones.
 Also: we have not “already built it”. The rules require the core to be built by the team at the
event.
FIX 2 — No photos of arrested hokims
 Arrest photos: presumption of innocence, defamation and copyright risk. Officials of the same
government sit on the jury. A pitch that looks like an attack on hokims gets rejected politely.
 Do not claim “the higher hokim is really responsible but hides it”. That is an accusation we
cannot prove.
 Use public court statistics instead (Section 2). Facts hit harder than photos.
FIX 3 — “Legality”, not “corruption”
 Corruption is for prosecutors and the Anti-Corruption Agency. Our job: does this decision match
the law?
 Our message: “We help hokimliks adopt lawful decisions the first time, and help Adliya catch
the rest before they hurt anyone.” This makes hokimlik lawyers our users, not our enemies.
FIX 4 — Not just a percentage
 A bare “73%” is useless to a lawyer and breaks the Ministry’s own AI ethics rules (No. 3787,
14.03.2026): decisions may not rely only on AI conclusions.
 Show a review-priority score plus every reason, with the lex.uz article and a quote, plus
approve/reject by a human expert.
FIX 5 — The money is support, not equity
 Official terms: winners get funds after forming an MChJ, paid in at least two tranches over the
year against performance indicators, disbursed by IT Park.
 So we pitch like a startup raising money: budget, milestones per tranche, KPIs, and what comes
after (Section 13). We also ask for something worth more than money: a pilot and data access.

2 · The problem
One sentence: good decisions are made at the top, but at district level hokim decisions are
often written against the law, and today they are checked mostly by hand, so many illegal
decisions reach citizens and are only cancelled later in court.
The numbers (all public)
Fact
Source
In 2024, administrative courts cancelled 933 decisions signed by regional,
district and city hokims as unlawful. Leaders: Tashkent region 143, Samarkand
128, Jizzakh 113.
Supreme Court press
service via Daryo
In 2023, courts cancelled 1,371 hokimlik decisions.

2025, by department: courts found 611 of 2,043 challenged hokimlik
decisions, 743 of 1,593 tax-authority decisions and 449 of 1,594 Cadastre
Agency decisions unlawful.
Supreme Court via Anhor

Over 2.5 years, 10,250 illegal decisions were cancelled on adliya

Kun.uz, 2022

resolved positively; the most social-media complaints concern law
enforcement, courts and hokimliks.
Daryo (President’s
meeting)
Why it happens (our insight)
• Mandate drift. A President’s decree or Cabinet decision sets a good goal. A district hokim
turns it into a local order that goes further than the law allows: extra duties, inspections
without legal grounds, land actions without a court. The decision cites the decree, but the
content does not match it.
• Thin legal capacity. Hokimlik lawyers and district adliya staff check drafts by hand against
34,000+ acts in force on lex.uz, under time pressure.
• Citizens are afraid and uninformed. A person affected by a local order rarely knows
whether it is lawful or where to complain.
Illustrative scenario (verify with our lawyer before using on stage): a district order, issued “to implement” a national
cleanliness campaign, sends officials with cameras into private homes without consent. Our citizen mode would explain
the relevant rights and draft a complaint to the district adliya office. Only present this as a real case if we find a
published one.
Why Adliya owns this problem
• Law on Normative Legal Acts, art. 22: draft decisions of local government are given legal
expertise by the relevant adliya bodies.
• Where a positive conclusion from the regional adliya body is required, the decision may not be
adopted without it.
• The Ministry monitors local law-making and reports to the Presidential Administration every
quarter.
• Infrastructure exists to plug into: E-qaror, the single electronic system for drafting, agreeing
and registering local government decisions.

10
Hackathon scope
Decisions that implement a PQ/PF decree, Cabinet decision or ministry order, starting with land
and property decisions (most harm, clearest law, best-documented cancellations). Checks 1, 5
and the citation validator are the must-haves; 2–4 and 6 come after they work.
The hero flow: parent vs child
Step
What the engine does
1 · Find the
parent
Extract cited acts from the preamble: “PQ-xxxx”, “PF-xxxx”, “VMQ xxx-son”, “…ijrosini
ta’minlash maqsadida”. Regex first, LLM for the rest.
2 · Right version
Fetch the parent text from lex.uz (or e-qaror-respublika for ministry orders) as it read on
the decision date.
3 · Action lists
Turn both into lists: who must do what, by when, to whom, with what sanction.
4 · Compare
Every child action must trace to the parent. Flag additions (new duties, inspections,
fees, bans), distortions (stricter, different target), omissions (skipped protections:
consent, compensation, deadlines).
5 · Parent alive?
If the parent was repealed or amended, the decision rests on nothing.
6 · Show
Side-by-side view: parent clause left, child clause right, the drift highlighted in red with
the source.
Demo line: “The decree says X. The district order says X plus Y. Y appears in no law. MUVOFIQ
marks Y in red and shows both texts.” The jury understands in five seconds, and we accuse no
one.
