# Anti-corruption expertise: official figures, newest first (verified 19.11.2026 in the articles themselves)

Say the year with every number. Figures cover national and local drafts together unless stated; we have no local-only
figure, so say "drafts", never "hokim drafts".

## 2025: national anti-corruption report (Anti-Corruption Agency, considered by the Senate)
Source: kun.uz, 10.08.2026, https://kun.uz/ru/news/2026/08/10/v-2025-godu-bolshe-vsego-korrupsionnyx-prestupleniy-vyyavleno-v-sfere-obrazovaniya-zdravooxraneniya-i-v-xokimiyatax
- "Каждый четвёртый проект нормативно-правового акта содержал коррупциогенные факторы" (every fourth draft act).
- Most corruption crimes: "дошкольного и школьного образования", "здравоохранения, местных хокимиятов и коммерческих
  банков" (local hokimiyats among the top sectors).

## 2024: national report (via the Senate review)
Source: uzdaily.uz, 05.09.2025, https://www.uzdaily.uz/ru/senat-uzbekistana-rassmotrel-natsionalnyi-doklad-o-borbe-s-korruptsiei/
- "Министерством юстиции выявлено 337 коррупционных факторов в 222 из более чем 2 тысяч проектов нормативно-правовых
  актов" (about 11 %).

## H1 2024: Anti-Corruption Agency, ministry and agency drafts
Source: aca.uz, 12.08.2024, https://aca.uz/oz/article/normativ-huquqiy-hujjatlar-va-ular-loyihalarining-korrupsiyaga-qarshi-ekspertizasi
- 186 drafts; "186 ta loyihaning 70 foizida (130 ta) 437 ta korrupsiyani keltirib chiqaruvchi omillar aniqlanib".
- Most frequent factors (O'RQ-860 checklist numbers in brackets): contradictions 83 [3], missing or incomplete
  administrative procedures 55 [14], vague and unfounded references 39 [2], norms allowing auctions to be avoided 38
  [23], broad discretionary powers 35 [4]. This ranking sets Mezon's build order: [3] and [14] need the local model;
  [2] needs a tighter rule than the one dropped on 19.11; [23] had no hits in our texts; [4] is a kept rule.

## History only (2021-22, before O'RQ-860 of 08.08.2023)
Source: yuz.uz, 19.04.2022 (Ministry press service). To end-2021: 708 of 1,820 drafts (39 %) came with no checklist;
245 (13 %) had 405 factors. Q1 2022: 34 drafts returned unreviewed for a missing checklist. Plan: all 21,000+ acts in
force examined by 2026.

Not verified, never use: "293 self-reviewed drafts later showed 457 factors".

## 2025: administrative court results (Supreme Court press service)
Source: anhor.uz, 21.01.2026, https://anhor.uz/uzl/news/2025-yilda-mansabdor-shaxslarning/ (verified 19.11.2026).
- "mahalliy hokimliklarning 2 043 ta qaroridan 611 tasi, soliq organlarining 1 593 ta qaroridan 743 tasi" "hamda
  Kadastr agentligining 1 594 ta qaroridan 449 tasi qonunga zid deb topilgan". The 611 of 2,043 matches stat.sud.uz
  exactly (research/scoreboard.md): two independent official sources.
